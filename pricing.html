FAQ · 006

The questions, answered.

N.01

What does "lifetime updates" mean?

Every update. Forever. One purchase covers every future version at no extra charge.

N.02

Can I switch tiers?

The Student tier requires a .edu email at purchase. The Team tier can be upgraded for more seats at any time. The Founder tier just refers to the price you paid at launch.

N.03

What payment methods?

Card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and most regional methods supported by Stripe.

N.04

Do you offer purchase orders or invoicing?

Yes, for Team licenses. Email hello@auris.talk and we'll send a quote.

N.05

What about education or non-profit discounts beyond the Student tier?

The Student tier covers verified students. For other education or non-profit use, the regular Lifetime price applies. Volume discounts kick in at the Team tier.

N.06

What happens to my license if I switch computers?

The license follows you. Install on a new machine, enter your license key, you're back in.
