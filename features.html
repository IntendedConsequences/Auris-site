App-aware · 004

Polish per app.

Different settings switch in based on which app has focus.
Five modes, four dropdowns, plus free-text overrides.

Per-app modes.

Five preset modes: Default, Formal, Casual, Minimal, Email. Pick one per app and Auris polishes accordingly.

Four dropdowns per app.

Mode, tone, format, language. Stack them per app — defaults cover most, override the rest.

Per-app instructions.

Free-text override field. "Always sign with my name." "Never use exclamation points." Anything the dropdowns don't cover.

Auto-switch.

Auris reads which app has focus and applies its preset automatically. No manual selection.

Studio Mode.

Real files on disk.

No export step because there's nothing to export from.
