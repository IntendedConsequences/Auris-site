For · 001

What people do with Auris.

Five workflows, five ways Auris polishes what you say.

Easier on your hands.

Tap-and-release hands-free mode with auto-stop on silence. Useful when you have RSI, a sprained wrist, or just need a break from typing all day.

Writers drafting prose.

Memos save as real Markdown files on your disk. Sync them via Dropbox, iCloud, or git. Studio Mode disappears the UI for distraction-free long-form composition.

Slack, Gmail, Notion.

Auris reads the active app and switches tone. Casual for chat, professional for email, plain for notes. Set rules per app once, forget them.

English here, Spanish there.

Lock the transcription language per app. English in Slack, Spanish in personal Notes. Whisper picks the right one and drops the wrong one.

Sensitive work.

Built-in pattern redaction for email, phone, SSN, credit card, plus custom regex you add. Matched dictations never get saved to history. Or run the whole stack fully offline.

Run it · 005

Three ways to run Auris.

Auris doesn't host an AI. You bring your own, or run one on your own machine.

Bring your keys.

Use your OpenAI, Anthropic, or Groq account. Auris talks to them directly with your API key. Groq currently has a free tier. No payment method needed to start.

Fully local.

Run a local LLM with Ollama, LM Studio, llama.cpp, or anything that speaks the OpenAI API. You pay nothing past your electric bill. Audio never leaves your machine.

Mixed.

Local for sensitive work, health notes, legal drafts, internal codebases. Cloud when you want the heavy hitters like Claude or ChatGPT. Switch in Settings any time.
