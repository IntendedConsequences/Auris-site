Setup

Your speech goes one of two places.

Pick one when you set Auris up. Switch any time. Both work the same from your side. The difference is whether anything touches a network.
Cloud · your own account

Through your API key.

The polish step runs through your account at OpenAI, Anthropic, or Groq. Your transcript (and audio, if you also chose cloud speech-to-text) goes from your machine to the provider you chose, then back.

Auris never sees it.
The provider's privacy policy applies to that hop. Speech-to-text can independently be set to local, in which case audio never leaves your machine even on this path.

Cost · most people pay $2 to $4/month
Local · no internet needed

On your machine.

The polish step runs locally through Ollama, LM Studio, or any compatible local server. Nothing leaves your computer.

No API calls. No network. Works on a plane.

Or skip the LLM entirely. Auris ships with built-in rules-based polish — capitalization, spacing, and common fixes. Lighter touch than an LLM, but it works with zero setup and zero dependencies.

Cost · $0 in API fees

Sensitive

Sensitive content stays out of your history.

Auris ships with four built-in patterns. When you dictate something that matches, the value lands in the app you're typing in, but doesn't get saved to your transcript history.
Email

Anything matching an email pattern.
Never saved to your transcript history.

Phone

US and international phone numbers. Lands in the app, never in history.

SSN

Social Security Numbers.
Used in the moment, not stored.

Credit card

16-digit card numbers, all major schemes. Typed once, gone after.

How redaction works. Patterns match locally after the transcript returns, so cloud STT or cloud LLM polish sees unredacted content first; only your local history gets filtered. Add your own regex for company IDs, project codes, anything else. Pre-send redaction for cloud paths is on the roadmap.

Guarantees

What we stand behind.

Four claims, four proofs.
No marketing fluff, no buzzwords. Just plain proof.

On-Device Audio

Local Whisper keeps audio on-device. Bytes opened, processed, discarded in RAM. If you choose cloud STT (Groq, etc.), audio goes to the provider only.

No Telemetry

Zero analytics, zero crash reports, zero usage stats. No opt-out switch because there's nothing to opt out of.

BYOK or Local

Bring your Anthropic, OpenAI, or Groq key. Or run a local LLM for full privacy. Auris never proxies your keys.

Open Formats

Memos as .md, history as .jsonl, dictionary as .json. Nothing locked inside a proprietary archive.

Your rights

Your data.
Your purchase.

01 · Your data

Yours alone.

Memos, dictations, and the dictionary live on your machine. Take them, move them, copy them, delete them. Nothing is locked inside Auris.

02 · Your purchase

Now and forever.

Your license is yours, now and forever. There's no server-side check that can turn it off, because there is no server. Keep it, transfer it, your choice.

03 · Your delete

One folder. Gone.

To erase everything Auris knows about you: delete the ~/Library/Auris/ folder on your Mac (or %APPDATA%\Auris\ on Windows).

FAQ

Questions

N.01

Do you sell or share data with anyone?

→

No. We don't have audio or transcript data to sell, and the email we hold for licensing isn't shared.

N.02

Where is my email stored?

→

In our customer database (Stripe for billing, a standard email provider for product updates).
Request deletion any time at hello@auris.talk.

N.03

Is Auris GDPR-compliant?

→

The architecture makes most of GDPR easy. We don't process audio. We don't store transcripts. The data we do hold (email, license key) is subject to standard subject-access and deletion requests.
Email hello@auris.talk to request either.

N.04

Can I see what's in my local history file?

→

Yes. Use the in-app History tab to view, edit, or delete entries.

N.05

What about telemetry or crash reports?

→

No automatic crash reports. Internal diagnostics live in Settings → Diagnostics. Review them on your machine, send them to us manually if you'd like our help. Never sent without your action.

N.06

What about the waitlist email forms on this site?

→

The marketing site uses a third-party form service for the launch waitlist email captures, so those emails route through that provider before reaching us. The Auris app itself routes nothing through us, ever. Different rules for the brochure than for the product.
