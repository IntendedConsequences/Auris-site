Do I need to set anything up after I buy?

Two minutes. The first time you open Auris, a setup walks you through giving microphone access and picking where Auris should process your speech: your own API key at OpenAI, Anthropic, or Groq, or kept entirely on your machine. Pick one, click through, start talking.

N.02

What does the API usage actually cost?

Most people pay $2 to $5 a month.
Go local instead and the only cost is electricity.

N.03

What if I don't like it?

30-day money-back, no questions.
Reply with the word "refund" and we'll process it within 24 hours.

N.04

Is there a free trial?

The 30-day money-back is the closest thing. Every tier gives you the full app.
Buy it, use it for a month, refund if it isn't worth $79.

N.05

Will it run on my computer?

If you're on Mac or Windows from this decade, yes.
Linux is on the way, not in the box yet.

N.06

How accurate is it?

Accurate enough that most people stop noticing they're dictating.
If a word it doesn't know shows up wrong, fix it once and Auris remembers.
